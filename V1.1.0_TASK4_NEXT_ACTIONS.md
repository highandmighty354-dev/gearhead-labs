# V1.1.0 Task 4 — Remaining Certification Gates

The following are the only remaining gates before Task 4 can be marked fully certified:

1. **Independent known-answer vectors:** each calculator receives at least one independently derived benchmark; exact/derived outputs must meet a predeclared tolerance, while model/advisory tools require documented assumptions and acceptance ranges.
2. **Units/conversion matrix:** every calculator exposing unit conversion receives independent round-trip and direct-conversion checks.
3. **Boundary matrix:** denominators, zero values, negative values, physical-domain limits, and empty/default states are tested according to calculator type.
4. **Regression matrix:** every repaired formula is rechecked directly and through calculators that consume its result.
5. **Live browser:** the published build is opened in a real browser and the production navigation, category expansion, calculator rendering, inputs, outputs, and error states are exercised.

No certification badge should be published until all five gates are closed.
